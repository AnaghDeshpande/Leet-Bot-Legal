Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how DiscordLeetBot (the “Bot”) handles information when it posts coding-problem and programming-contest announcements to Discord.

Information the Bot uses

The Bot uses the configured Discord channel ID to know where to post. While connected to Discord, it may receive technical Discord information required to operate, such as server, channel, message, and thread identifiers. The current Bot does not provide user accounts, profiles, analytics, or user tracking.

Information stored by the Bot

To avoid duplicate announcements, the Bot stores a small local JSON file containing identifiers of announcements it has already sent. This file contains contest or problem identifiers only; it does not intentionally store Discord messages, user names, user IDs, email addresses, or other personal profile information.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the Bot: posting announcements to the configured channel, creating associated discussion threads, and preventing repeat announcements. The Bot does not sell, rent, or use information for advertising.

Third-party services

The Bot connects to Discord and may retrieve public contest or problem information from LeetCode, Codeforces, and CodeChef. Those services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. The Bot does not control their practices.

Retention and security

Announcement identifiers are retained only as long as needed to prevent duplicate posts and may be pruned during normal operation. Reasonable measures are used to protect the Bot’s configuration and access token, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

A server administrator can remove the Bot from a server at any time. If you have questions about data associated with a particular deployment, contact the Bot operator through the project’s official repository or the Discord server where the Bot is provided.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.
